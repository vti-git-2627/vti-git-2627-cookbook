# Fluffy pancakes

**Serves:** 4 · **Time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 200 g flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- a pinch of salt
- 2 eggs
- 300 ml milk
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

## Steps

1. Mix flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a bowl.
2. Whisk eggs, milk and melted butter in another bowl.
3. Pour the wet mix into the dry mix and stir until just combined. Lumps are fine.
4. Heat a pan on medium heat with a little butter.
5. Pour a small ladle of batter per pancake. Flip when bubbles appear on top.

## Tip

Let the batter rest for 10 minutes before baking: the pancakes rise higher.
